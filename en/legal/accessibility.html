Accessibility

The current release incorporates keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive alternative text, reduced motion support, and semantic markup. Real-world compatibility may vary depending on browser, device, and assistive technology.

Enhancements and Feedback

To report an accessibility barrier, please email kingdombrandbqto@gmail.com indicating the specific page, your device/browser setup, and a description of the issue. This feedback is used to prioritize continuous improvements.

Framework and Goals

Kingdom utilizes WCAG standards as our ongoing engineering reference; no public claim of compliance with a specific level or version is made until a formal verifiable audit is conducted.
