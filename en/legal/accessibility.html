Accessibility

Kingdom is dedicated to providing an accessible digital experience for all visitors, aligning technical implementation with recognized web accessibility standards.

1. Goal and Target Standards

Our ongoing technical objective is conformance with WCAG 2.1 AA (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). This platform incorporates sequential keyboard navigation, clear visible focus outlines, semantic markup, high-contrast text ratios, and full respect for reduced-motion user preferences (prefers-reduced-motion).

In accordance with principles of technical truthfulness, no claim of absolute 100% compliance or formal certification is made until a comprehensive, third-party verifiable audit is completed.

2. Known Limitations

We actively monitor and work to resolve the following known technical constraints:

  • Certain legacy portfolio images display multi-element vector artwork whose descriptive alternative text is undergoing continuous refinement.
  • Graffiti-styled decorative display typography is strictly restricted to primary section titles, ensuring all body text renders using the accessible sans-serif font Montserrat.

3. Feedback and Reporting Access Barriers

If you experience any accessibility barrier or navigation difficulty on this website, please contact Ramses Martínez directly:

Accessibility Support Email: kingdombrandbqto@gmail.com

Please specify the webpage URL, your device and browser or assistive technology, along with a description of the barrier so we can address it promptly.
