Intellectual Property and Portfolio

1. Kingdom Brand & Creative Authorship

Trade names, logos, distinctive emblems, mockups, and original graphic compositions of Kingdom belong to its creator, Ramses Martínez, under Venezuelan and international intellectual property law. Title to the studio's creations is supported by design master files, original digital matrices, and corresponding merchant records.

2. Distinction Between Client Trademarks and Apparel Design Authorship (Venezuelan Legal Grounds)

When developing sublimated apparel, corporate or sports uniforms, and computerized embroidery digitizing, Kingdom strictly distinguishes two independent legal spheres under the laws of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela:

  • Distinctive Signs Supplied by the Client (Industrial Property Law): The client retains ownership or licensing rights over pre-existing word marks, figures, and logos provided for incorporation into manufactured goods.
  • Authorship of Textile Graphic Design and Composition (Copyright Law / Ley sobre el Derecho de Autor): Pursuant to Articles 1, 2, 5, and 6 of the Venezuelan Copyright Law, all works of the intellect of a creative nature—including illustration, color palette engineering, vector scale layout, volumetry, and aesthetic garment adaptation—constitute protected works whose original author is Ramses Martínez (Kingdom) by the sole fact of creation.
  • Absence of Implied Assignment of Economic Rights: Under Article 51 et seq. of the Venezuelan Copyright Law, any assignment or transfer of economic exploitation rights must be interpreted restrictively and must be expressly documented in writing. The mere purchase of garment confection, printing, or textile yardage does not transfer intellectual property nor copyright assignment of the garment design if the client has not contracted, remunerated, and formalized an express copyright assignment agreement for the custom graphic artwork.
  • Inalienable Moral Rights & Portfolio Exhibition: Under Articles 18 and 19 of the Venezuelan Copyright Law, the author retains the inalienable and unwaivable moral right to claim paternity of the creation and to disclose or showcase the work. Consequently, displaying technical design concepts in this portfolio constitutes the lawful exercise of the author's right to disclose professional works and technical craftsmanship.

3. Third-Party Content, Franchises, and Tribute Works (Fan Art)

Certain portfolio showcases present conceptual illustrations or mockups inspired by entertainment franchises, characters, or automotive marks (such as anime, pop culture, or vehicle studies). These pieces are classified with conspicuous badges denoting Fan Art, Graphic Study, or Artistic Tribute, are devoid of pricing, ordering, or commercial call-to-actions, and are displayed strictly as technical and artistic demonstrations without implying commercial association, sponsorship, or licensing from their respective rights holders.

4. Materials Supplied by Clients and Indemnification Duty

In accordance with contractual good faith principles, clients warrant that they possess ownership, express authorization, or a valid license to reproduce logos, fonts, and emblems supplied to Kingdom for textile orders. Clients assume full liability against third-party claims arising from unauthorized materials provided.

5. Amicable Notification and Takedown Procedure

If any client or rights holder believes in good faith that a piece, image, or reference displayed in this portfolio should be modified, anonymized, or removed, they may submit a formal request to kingdombrandbqto@gmail.com, identifying the item and applicable grounds. Kingdom will diligently review justified communications to resolve display matters amicably.
