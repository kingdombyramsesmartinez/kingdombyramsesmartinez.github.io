Cookies and Similar Technologies

1. Technologies Used on this Site

The Kingdom website uses strictly necessary technical local storage to record your consent preference (key kingdom_cookie_consent in localStorage).

In addition and on a purely optional basis, the site integrates Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-YQCLS51XJE) to collect aggregated and anonymous visit metrics. Google Analytics is neither downloaded, initialized, nor installs cookies or identifiers until the user explicitly clicks "Accept" in the consent notice.

2. Real Cookies Inventory

When the user provides express consent, Google Analytics 4 may set the following technical and analytical cookies in the browser:

Cookie: _ga
Provider: Google LLC (Google Analytics 4)
Purpose: Distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated pseudonymous identifier to calculate visitor, session, and campaign metrics.
Duration: 2 years from creation or update.

Cookie: _ga_YQCLS51XJE (or _ga_*)
Provider: Google LLC (Google Analytics 4)
Purpose: Persists and synchronizes session state for the configured measurement stream.
Duration: 2 years from creation or update.

Local Storage: kingdom_cookie_consent
Provider: First-party (Kingdom)
Purpose: Stores user consent choice (accept or decline) to prevent re-displaying the banner during subsequent visits.
Duration: Persistent until cleared by user or consent is revoked.

3. Managing and Withdrawing Consent

You can update, withdraw, or alter your consent at any time via the direct link available in the footer of every page on this website:

Clicking "Decline" or revoking consent clears the storage record and stops any analytical measurement scripts for the session immediately.

5. Absence of Advertising Cookies or Profiling

This website does not employ remarketing cookies, third-party advertising networks, commercial tracking pixels, or automated behavioral user profiling.
