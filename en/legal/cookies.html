Cookies and Similar Technologies

1. Technologies Used on this Site

The Kingdom website utilizes strictly necessary technical local storage to record your cookie consent preferences (via the key kingdom_cookie_consent in localStorage).

In addition and on a purely optional basis, the site integrates Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-YQCLS51XJE) to collect aggregated and anonymous navigation metrics (pages visited, device type, duration of visit). Google Analytics is neither initialized nor installs cookies or identifiers until the user explicitly clicks "Accept" in the privacy and cookies notice.

2. Choice and Managing Preferences

When accessing the website for the first time, a notice is displayed with clear choices to accept or decline analytics collection. If you choose "Decline", no analytics scripts are downloaded and no tracking is performed.

You can review or change your choice at any time using the direct link: Configure cookie settings or by clearing this site's storage in your browser privacy preferences.

3. Links to External Services

When interacting with links to WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or Google Drive, you are redirected to third-party platforms. Those providers manage their own cookies, pixels, and privacy policies under their respective terms.

4. Absence of Advertising Cookies or Profiling

This website does not use behavioral advertising cookies, remarketing networks, ad tracking pixels, or commercial user profiling tools.
